## Opening Song: "Alas, and Did My Savior Bleed"

> *[KEEP ORIGINAL HYMN TEXT — do not rewrite]*

**What this song means:**

This is an old song about Jesus dying on the cross.

The writer asks: "Did Jesus really die for me?"

He is amazed that Jesus — who is holy — would die for sinful people.

He feels deep thankfulness. He wants to give his whole life to God.

## PART 1: GOD'S LONGING

Every person wants to be truly known.

We want others to see us as we really are.

Sometimes we hide who we really are — like wearing a mask. It is difficult to get to know someone who is wearing a mask.

When someone has a wrong view of us, we cannot have a real friendship with them.

This is also true with God.

**God's longing is this: he wants us to know him as he truly is.**

## PART 2: THE MISSING PICTURE

Many people have different ideas about God.

But one idea is often missing: **God is holy.**

Holiness is very important. We must understand it to know who God really is.

Without understanding holiness, we will have a wrong view of God. Our Christian life will also be weak.

If we do not understand holiness, the cross is confusing. We will not be able to understand why Jesus had to suffer and die.

---

**Quote — R.C. Sproul, *Holiness of God*:**

> "The most important idea I found in the Bible is that God is holy. It is basic to understanding God and Christianity. The Bible says that God's name is holy — because he is holy."

*(This is a simplified version of the original quote.)*

The Bible also says: "Holy is his name" (Luke 1:49). His name is holy because he is holy.

The Bible sometimes repeats something three times. This shows it is very, very important.

In Isaiah 6, the seraphim say God is "holy, holy, holy."

This is the only quality of God repeated three times in the whole Bible.

God is not called "love, love, love." Not "powerful, powerful, powerful." Not "good, good, good."

Not just holy. Not holy, holy. He is holy, holy, holy.

Only **holy** is said three times.

This shows holiness is the most important thing to know about God.

## PART 3: THE MEANING OF HOLINESS

What does "holy" mean?

Many people think it means "pure" — having no sin at all.

This is true. But it is not the main meaning.

**The main meaning of "holy" is "separate."**

The original word means "to separate."

Here is a second meaning: God is also **transcendent**.

Transcendent means "far above everything else."

*Example: A king sits high on a throne, above all the people. He is higher, more powerful, and different from everyone in the room. Now imagine someone infinitely higher than the greatest king. That is a small picture of God's transcendence.*

Think of it this way: God is not just a little greater than us. He is in a completely different category above us. God is above the whole world.

---

**Quote — R.C. Sproul, *Holiness of God*:**

> "God is separate from us. He is above us. He is greater than all things. When the Bible calls God holy, it means he is different from us in a special way. He is far above everything."

*(This is a simplified version of the original quote.)*
