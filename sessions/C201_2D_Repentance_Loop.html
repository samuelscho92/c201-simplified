SESSION 2D — CONFESSION AND REPENTANCE LOOP

Course 201 · Chapter 2 · Repentance

Key Words

confession honestly naming your sin before God
intercession praying for other people

Introduction: Why Confession Matters

Quote — Henri Nouwen, The Only Necessary Thing:

"Evil loses its power when we confess that we have been in its control."

(Simplified from original)

Quote — Richard Foster, Celebration of Discipline:

"Without the cross, confession would only help us feel better emotionally. But it does so much more. It changes our relationship with God objectively. It heals and transforms us from the inside."

(Simplified from original)

An important warning:

A general confession ("I'm sorry for all my sins") may help us avoid shame. But it will not bring deep healing.

Confession should be specific.

Name the actual sin — pride, anger, fear, selfishness. Be honest and concrete.

Quote — Richard Foster, Celebration of Discipline:

"It is easy to avoid real guilt in a general confession. We need to confess specific sins — sins of the heart like pride, greed, and anger — as well as sins in our actions."

(Simplified from original)

Another important warning:

There must be an end point to confession.

Confession begins with sorrow — but it ends with joy.

God forgives us. There is celebration. We must not stay forever in self-criticism.

Quote — J.D. Greear, Stop Asking Jesus Into Your Heart:

"Telling a friend or pastor about your sin and hearing them say 'it's OK' may feel good. But their words cannot restore your relationship with God. Only Jesus can do that. Our tears do not wash away sin. Only Jesus' blood does."

(Simplified from original)

The Repentance Loop

This is a practical tool for bringing your sin to God.

2 Timothy 3:16–17 (NLT)

All Scripture is inspired by God and is useful to teach us what is true and to make us realize what is wrong in our lives. It corrects us when we are wrong and teaches us to do what is right. God uses it to prepare and equip his people to do every good work.

All Scripture teaches us, corrects us, and trains us to live rightly.

X = an event, a reaction, a choice, or a temptation that you need to stop and consider.

Step 1 — Observe and Reflect

Stop. Look carefully at what happened.

Ask yourself: - What exactly happened? - What was I feeling? - Why did I react that way? - What was going through my mind?

We must see our sin clearly. We cannot repent of what we refuse to see.

Step 2 — Word of God

Bring the situation to the Bible.

Ask: - What does the gospel say to me here? - What is true according to God's word? - How does the Bible correct me in this situation?

This is the step that separates Christian repentance from simply feeling bad.

Without God's word, you leave God out of the process.

Step 3 — Confess and Apologize

Be specific. Avoid vague language.

Name the sin clearly: "I was proud." "I was afraid and did not trust God." "I was selfish."

Then apologize — to God, and to any person you have hurt.

Express your trust in God's forgiveness through the cross of Jesus.

Step 4 — Commit

Repentance means "turning around."

Express your new direction in words: - What do you now believe? - What do you commit to do differently? - How does the gospel speak to this area of your life?

If you stop at confession without committing, the sin will likely return.

Step 5 — Plan of Action

Make a specific plan.

Ask: - What concrete steps will I take? - Who can help me with this? - Who should I bring into this for accountability?

Apply It

Think about the past week.

Choose one incident or area where the Holy Spirit is showing you sin.

Write out your repentance using the 5 steps above.

Be honest. Be specific. End with the gospel — what God says is true about you because of Jesus.

Chapter 2 Summary:

  • 2A — We Need Repentance: Repentance is the path to fellowship with God.
  • 2B — Dreadful Repentance: Pride, self-atonement, and not trusting grace make repentance hard.
  • 2C — We Don't Understand Repentance: Repentance is not gloomy, negative, or rare. It brings refreshing.
  • 2D — Repentance Loop: Use the 5-step loop to bring specific sin to God regularly.
